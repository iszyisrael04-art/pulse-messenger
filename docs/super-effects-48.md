# Original Pulse super effects

Eight original abstract effects replace the old cinema collection, alongside Pulse Universe. Animal gifts (including Butterfly) and the previous actor/car cinema SKUs are disabled for new sends. Old balances, receipts, events and assets remain so historical sends do not break. Other everyday gifts remain available.

The movies are original procedural screen effects: electric filaments, projected orbital geometry, portals, glass shards, aurora ribbons, accretion disks and expanding shockwaves. They use layered bloom, colour separation, vignette, smooth fades and original synthesized stereo sound. These are pre-rendered gift visuals, not face tracking or editable camera filters.

Render command: `python scripts/render_super_effects.py --output /tmp/pulse-super-effects` (numpy, Pillow, ffmpeg). Dimensions 480×854, 24fps, 144 frames, 6 seconds, H.264/AAC. New media plus thumbnails totals 7.30 MB. Source, deterministic seeds and full byte receipts are included. No third-party models, recordings or generation providers are used.

New effects cost 29,999–99,999 normal coins. New effect rewards follow the existing newer-cinema ratio of 0.05 P-coins per coin; other gift prices/payouts are unchanged. No purchases or test credits are made by this update.

Shop has a free local Preview button with sound, replay and close controls; it does not call a send/payment RPC. The browser Shop also previews without sign-in or native app installation. Reduced-motion fallback, muted playback, safe background pause and queue deadlines are preserved. Device LIVE playback still requires acceptance testing.
